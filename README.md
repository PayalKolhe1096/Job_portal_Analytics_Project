🧠 Job Analytics Portal

📌 Project Overview

The Job Analytics Portal is a Tableau-based project designed to analyze and visualize job market data across roles, experience levels, and countries. The project evolved in two phases — initial training dashboards and enhanced internship dashboards — both created on the same dataset to build a complete analytics portal.

🎯 Objective

   -To analyze and visualize job postings data to uncover:

   -Global distribution of job roles

   -Salary patterns by role and country

   -Demand comparison across industries

   -Gender preferences and experience trends

   -Portal-specific job activity insights

📊 Dataset
The dataset used for this project is available on Kaggle:  
🔗 [Job Description Dataset – Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ravindrasinghrana/job-description-dataset)

This dataset was used for all dashboards and analytics tasks, including:
   - Salary and Experience Distribution  
   - Work Type Comparison  
   - Top Companies (Tree Map)  
   - India vs Germany Comparison  
   - Extra Dashboard from Training Phase  


🧩 Tools Used

   -Tableau Public – For building dashboards

   -Excel / CSV Dataset – For preprocessing data

   -GitHub – For project hosting and documentation

🚀 Phase 2: Internship Enhancements

As part of the internship, additional dashboards and analytics features were implemented on the same dataset to provide deeper and more business-oriented insights.

🔍 Added Dashboards:

   1.KPI Summary Dashboard – Overview of total postings, average salary, and active roles.

   2.Salary Distribution (Box Plot) – Focused view of salaries above $8,000 for major roles.

   3.Work Type Share (Pie Chart) – Breakdown of full-time vs part-time jobs.

   4.Top 10 Companies (Tree Map) – Shows hiring trends for Data Engineer and Data Scientist excluding Asian and “C” countries.

   5.India vs Germany Comparison (Stacked Bar) –
     Filtered on B.Tech, Full-Time, Female Preference, Salary > $10,000 (before 08/01/2023).

     Note: India had no matching records; hence only Germany data displayed.

   6.Experience vs Salary Trend – Correlation between experience years and salary levels.

Each dashboard extends the analytical depth and demonstrates advanced Tableau features such as filters, calculated fields, and custom notes.

💡 Key Insights

-Most high-paying jobs were concentrated in specific regions and roles.

-Full-time B.Tech positions with >2 years experience had limited female preference postings.

-India had no eligible postings under strict filters, while Germany showed notable presence in Art Teacher and Aerospace Engineer roles.
