🧠 Job Analytics Portal

📌 Project Overview

The Job Analytics Portal is a Tableau-based data visualization project designed to analyze and compare global job postings across multiple roles, countries, and work types. The dashboards provide key insights into hiring patterns, salary distributions, experience levels, and country-based job market trends.

🎯 Objective

-To analyze and visualize job postings data to uncover:

-Global distribution of job roles

-Salary patterns by role and country

-Demand comparison across industries

-Gender preferences and experience trends

-Portal-specific job activity insights

🧩 Tools Used

-Tableau Public – For building dashboards

-Excel / CSV Dataset – For preprocessing data

-GitHub – For project hosting and documentation

📊 Dashboards Included

Below are the key visual dashboards (screenshots) included in this project folder:

1.KPI Summary Dashboard – Overview of total postings, average salary, and active job roles.

2.Salary Distribution (Box Plot) – Distribution of salaries above $8,000 for various roles.

3.Work Type Share (Pie Chart) – Visualization of job share across full-time and part-time roles.

4.Top 10 Companies (Tree Map) – Companies hiring for “Data Engineer” and “Data Scientist” roles (non-Asian countries).

5.India vs Germany Comparison (Stacked Bar) – Role-wise comparison for B.Tech, Full-Time, Female preference postings.

6.Experience vs Salary Trend – Correlation between years of experience and average salary.

7.Each dashboard provides focused insights on different dimensions of the dataset.

💡 Key Insights

- Most high-paying jobs were concentrated in specific regions and roles.

-Full-time B.Tech positions with >2 years experience had limited female preference postings.

-India had no eligible postings under strict filters, while Germany showed notable presence in Art Teacher and Aerospace Engineer roles.
